# Product

## Positioning

**All Paws Inn** — 41 acres of restored 1938 rice-and-cattle land at Fulshear, Fort Bend County,
Texas. Forty minutes west of downtown Houston, twenty-five from the Energy Corridor. Boutique,
single property, independently owned, established 2016. Fictional.

The research showed the category's top tier splitting into two looks: cool urban ultra-luxury
(D Pet Hotels, Sniff Dog Hotel) and clean corporate franchise (K9 Resorts, Wag Hotels). Almost
nobody reads as *an actual place*. A country inn with land, live oaks and timber barns is the open
position, and it earns the material palette rather than decorating with it.

**Two houses, one inn.** The Barn (38 rooms, dogs) and The Cattery (24 rooms, cats) are co-equal
buildings, forty metres and an entire ventilation system apart. This is the spine of the whole
product: cat owners distrust dog-centric facilities, so a building no dog has entered is the
selling point rather than a compromise. Every shared surface — hero, room index, timeline, report
card, cameras, spa, kitchen — carries both houses.

**Local competitors.** The relocation put us among sites the research already ranked: Camp
Run-A-Mutt Houston Galleria, Meadowlake (Houston & Sugar Land), Rover Oaks (Houston & Katy), Paws
Pet Resort Houston, Dog's Day Inn Katy, K9 Resorts Sugar Land.

**Voice.** Composed, specific, unhurried. Speaks like a good innkeeper. Never says *kennel*,
*cage* or *facility* about itself — only about competitors, and only once, deliberately.

## Segments, ranked by value

## Conversions

- **Primary** — reservation request through the Stay Planner (`/plan`)
- **Secondary** — free meet & greet (`/new-guests`); the real first conversion for new customers
- **Tertiary** — hero date bar, phone, portal sign-in, packing checklist

### Gulf Coast specifics
Two trust features the location buys, and neither appears anywhere in the researched 50:
**storm and flood protocol** (`/safety#storms`) and a **summer heat protocol** (`/safety#heat`).
Heartworm prevention is a hard requirement rather than a suggestion — mosquitoes here have no
off-season, and it is the one thing that turns a booking away.

### Cut deliberately

| Cut | Why |
|---|---|
| Airport valet parking | We do the transfer instead; parking is not our business |
| Animated impact counters | Research flags gratuitous stats; the rescue partnership is stated plainly instead |
| Multi-location switcher | One property *is* the premium story |
| Tiered training programme | Scope bloat; the Quiet Wing covers behavioural depth better |
| Blog / resource hub | Filler; `/new-guests` carries the informational load |
| Discount pop-ups | Explicit research anti-pattern |

## Content rules

- No Lorem Ipsum anywhere; every string is written copy.
- Rates are internally consistent and drive real arithmetic (see `lib/pricing.ts`).
- Guest notes are one *specific observed detail* each — that is what proves attention.
- Food shown or named is safe for the species being fed. No grapes, onion, garlic or chocolate
  anywhere in the kitchen menu or its photography.
- Climate copy must read as Gulf Coast: heat and water, never heating and snow.
- **Cat daycare is deliberately not invented.** The journey stresses a cat more than the day does,
  and `/daycare` says so. Honest asymmetry beats false parity.
